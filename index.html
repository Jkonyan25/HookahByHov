    Questions? Answers.

    How long does catering last?

    Standard catering service is 4 hours from your listed start time. An attendant stays on site the whole time managing coals, changing bowls and keeping every hookah running. Need a longer session? Mention it in the notes and we'll quote the extension when we call.

    Why did my estimate go up by $200?

    Two catering surcharges can apply: a $200 holiday fee if your event falls on a recognized holiday, and a $200 same-day fee for bookings made for today. Both are shown as separate lines in your estimate so you always know what you're looking at. Neither applies to delivery orders.

    How is the catering price calculated?

    It's tiered. The first 10 hookahs are $100 each, hookahs 11 through 20 are $80 each, and everything from 21 up is $70 each. So 10 hookahs is $1,000, 20 hookahs is $1,800, and 25 hookahs is $2,150.

    How far do you deliver?

    Delivery is free across our local service area. Further out, a travel fee of $50 or $100 applies depending on the distance. Enter your address in the booking form and it'll tell you exactly which applies — or give us a call at 818-807-8008.

    Can I request a specific tobacco brand or flavor?

    Absolutely. In the first step of the booking form, tap Add a flavor request. Specialty tobacco is priced separately based on the amount requested and current market price — we'll confirm the exact cost when we contact you.

    Do I pay on the website?

    No. This site only sends us your request and estimate. Nothing is charged online — we contact you to confirm every detail, then handle payment directly.

    Is the estimate the final price?

    It's an accurate estimate of the standard rates and fees listed on this page. The final total is confirmed when we speak — specialty tobacco, extended hours or unusual venue requirements are quoted at that point.

    Is there an age requirement?

    Yes. Hookah is a tobacco product — everyone using our hookahs must be 21 or older, and we may check ID on arrival.
